1. What the app does

GhostExif removes metadata from photos and videos you select. It writes a cleaned copy; the original is left in place unless you explicitly delete it through the app. The app is a utility, not a backup service: keep your own copies of anything you cannot lose.

2. Plans and purchases

  • Free plan: photo cleaning in batches of up to a fixed number of files per run, with a daily action limit that rewarded videos can extend.
  • Pro: removes the daily limit and unlocks video cleaning. Pro is sold through Google Play as a lifetime purchase and/or subscription products; the price, billing period and renewal terms are the ones Google Play shows before you confirm.
  • Purchases are tied to your Google Play account. Restore purchases re-applies what that account owns. Refunds, plan changes and cancellations are handled by Google Play under its own policy.
  • An offline licence is time-limited by a trusted clock; the app may need one network check to renew it. Cleaning keeps working while that check succeeds.
