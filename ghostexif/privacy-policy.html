GhostExif — Privacy Policy

Applies to the GhostExif Android app · Effective 5 November 2026 · Version 1.0

GhostExif is an offline-first privacy tool that removes sensitive metadata (GPS coordinates, camera serial numbers, embedded thumbnails, XMP fields, PNG chunks and MP4/MOV container atoms) from photos and videos. Everything is processed on your device: the files you pick are never uploaded, and no user account is created. This policy explains exactly what stays on your device and what third-party advertising and purchase libraries collect.

1. What we never collect or transmit

  • Your photos, videos or files — processed entirely on this device in memory and local storage. We never upload or receive them.
  • Location data — GhostExif strips GPS location; it never requests the device location permission and never transmits coordinates.
  • Metadata values — Exif, XMP, IPTC, and container atoms are read only for on-screen inspection and local cleaning; none are sent to any remote server.

The app never uploads any of the above. When you choose to share a cleaned file via Android's Share Sheet, that transfer is directed entirely by you to the target application you choose.

2. What we do not collect

No user account, name, email address, phone number, contacts, gallery album listings or browsing history. The app does not create a profile of you.

4. Permissions and why they exist

  • Android System Picker (Photo Picker / SAF) — you choose which media to inspect or clean. The app requests no broad storage permission (READ/WRITE/MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) and uses only the temporary URI grants provided by the system picker.
  • Notifications (Android 13+) — shows progress and completion of batch cleaning jobs in the background.
  • Foreground service (data sync) — ensures batch scrubbing of large video files completes without interruption if the screen turns off.
  • Internet, network state — strictly for rewarded ads, purchase verification, and license validation. Media scrubbing operations work fully offline.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Cleaned copies are written to the app's private sandbox, or to the public media collection (Pictures/Movies) so your gallery can see them, depending on your settings. Uninstalling GhostExif removes its private storage and preferences.
  • Original files remain untouched unless you explicitly choose to delete them through the app's media deletion prompt, which delegates to Android's system MediaStore delete confirmation.
  • Ad-related data is retained by Google according to your Google ad settings; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Purchase records are retained by Google Play (and RevenueCat) as required for billing and tax obligations.

Deletion requests. Because the app holds your content only on your device, the fastest deletion is uninstalling it; nothing else needs to be asked of us. For customer purchase records on RevenueCat, you can request deletion through the contact channel in section 10.

6. Your controls

  • Turn off ad personalization (Android Settings > Privacy > Ads), or simply do not watch the optional rewarded ads.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play > Subscriptions.
  • Delete local data by uninstalling the app. There is no server-side copy to erase.

7. Children

GhostExif is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. International users

The app itself transfers no personal data to us. The SDKs listed in section 3 process data in the regions described in their own privacy policies. Where local law grants you rights of access, correction or deletion, those rights apply to the data held by those providers; the app holds your content only on your device.
