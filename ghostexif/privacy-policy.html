GhostExif — Privacy Policy

Applies to the GhostExif Android app · Effective <DATE> · Version 1.0

GhostExif removes sensitive metadata (GPS coordinates, camera serial numbers, embedded thumbnails and other Exif/XMP fields) from photos and videos. Everything is processed on your device: the files you pick are never uploaded, and no account is created.

1. What GhostExif reads

  • The files you pick. You choose each file through Android's system picker, so the app sees only what you select. GhostExif asks for no storage permission.
  • The metadata inside those files (Exif, XMP, PNG chunks, MP4/MOV atoms) and, for a video, its container structure.
  • Your settings (save-to-gallery, the "don't ask again" answer, the licence state and the daily action counter), stored locally in the app's private storage.

2. What leaves your device

Nothing leaves the device for the cleaning itself. The app contacts the network only for these four purposes, and only when they apply:

Rewarded ads on the Free plan (opt-in, never a gate) Ad request data handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK Google AdMob

Trusted clock (so a Free action cannot be re-granted by changing the device date) An HTTP request whose Date header is read; no personal data The time server you reach
Error report — only when you choose to send one The diagnostic text you see before sending The destination shown in the report screen

Ad and purchase SDKs may collect device and advertising identifiers under their own policies (Google AdMob, RevenueCat, Google Play). GhostExif itself does not build a profile and has no server that stores your photos.

3. What is never collected

  • Your photos, videos or any copy of them.
  • File names, album contents or metadata values beyond the on-screen inspection you asked for.
  • Location. GhostExif removes GPS data; it never requests the location permission.
  • Contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, microphone or camera.

4. Permissions

Only INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (for the four purposes above). Files are opened one at a time through the system picker, so no broad storage permission is requested.

5. Retention and deletion

Cleaned copies are written where you chose: the app's private storage, or — from Android 10 — the Pictures/Movies folder so the gallery can see them. The originals stay untouched unless you delete them through the app's own prompt, which uses Android's media delete. Uninstalling the app removes its private storage and settings.

6. Children

GhostExif is a utility for general audiences and does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes and the new text ships with the app.
