PrivaVault — Privacy Policy

Applies to the PrivaVault Android app · Effective 27 October 2026 · Version 1.0

PrivaVault is an offline-first privacy tool: a secure encrypted vault and a multi-pass file shredder. It has no user accounts, no cloud sync and no server that stores your content. This policy explains exactly what stays on your device and what the third-party advertising and purchase libraries collect.

1. What we never collect or transmit

  • Vault content — files you import are encrypted on this device (AES-256-GCM inside a local .pvault container). We never receive them.
  • Shredder targets — the files you select are overwritten and deleted locally; no copy, name or hash is transmitted.
  • Your PIN, recovery phrase and encryption keys — derived and stored locally (Android Keystore); we cannot read them and cannot recover them for you.
  • Shred certificates — generated locally; written out only when you pick a destination.

The app never uploads any of the above. Two features can move data at your request: a certificate/archive export (you choose the destination, which may be a cloud drive) and the device-to-device migration (section 4a, local network only). Those are your actions, not ours.

2. What we do not collect

No account, name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos library listing, vault file names or vault file content. The app does not create a profile of you.

About the location permission. On Android 12 and older, Android itself requires the fine location permission before an app may start a local-only Wi-Fi hotspot. PrivaVault asks for it only when you start a device-to-device migration and it never calls the location APIs: we do not read, store or transmit your device position. The ad SDK is a separate matter — it derives an approximate location from your IP address, as described in section 3. On Android 13+ the app uses the nearby-Wi-Fi permission instead, declared with the platform's neverForLocation flag.

4. Permissions and why they exist

  • Storage Access Framework picker — you choose which files to shred or import. The app has no broad storage permission and uses only the per-file grants the system hands back; those grants are not persisted after the operation.
  • Notifications (Android 13+) — progress and completion of a shred or import, including when the app is in the background.
  • Internet, network state — ads, purchase verification, and the peer-to-peer migration socket. Vault and shredder operations work fully offline.
  • NFC — a short tap to exchange a small pairing handshake between the two devices during a migration. No payment, tag or external NFC data is involved.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi / Wi-Fi state — starting or joining the local-only hotspot that carries the migration stream between your two devices. The stream never leaves your local network and no server is involved.
  • Foreground service (data sync) + wake lock — so a shred, import or migration that you started finishes even if the screen turns off.

4a. Device-to-device migration

When you choose to move your vault to a new phone, the two devices pair over NFC, one of them opens a local-only Wi-Fi hotspot, and the encrypted vault is streamed directly to the other device over that local network. Nothing passes through us or any third party; both devices must be physically present and the transfer is started by you.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Everything the app stores lives in its private app storage. Uninstalling PrivaVault removes it. Shredded files are destroyed permanently and cannot be recovered.
  • Ad-related data is retained by Google according to your Google ad settings; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Purchase records are retained by Google Play (and RevenueCat) as required for billing and tax obligations.

Deletion requests. Because the app holds your content only on your device, the fastest deletion is uninstalling it; nothing else needs to be asked of us. For the limited data described in section 3 you can request deletion through the contact channel in section 10, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings at any time.

6. Your controls

  • Turn off ad personalization (Android Settings → Privacy → Ads), or simply do not use the optional rewarded ads — the app is fully usable without them.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play → Subscriptions.
  • Delete all local data by uninstalling the app. There is no server-side copy to erase.

7. Children

PrivaVault is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. International users

The app itself transfers no personal data to us. The SDKs listed in section 3 process data in the regions described in their own privacy policies. Where local law grants you rights of access, correction or deletion, those rights apply to the data held by those providers; the app holds your content only on your device.
